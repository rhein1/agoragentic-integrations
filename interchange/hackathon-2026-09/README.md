## Confirmed submission rules

The owner's September 14 screenshots of Runtime's form establish:

- Submit by **Saturday, September 19, 2026 at 4 PM EDT** (20:00 UTC).
- Demos begin at **5 PM EDT** (21:00 UTC), New York time.
- Every submitted project automatically enters the Bankr grand prize. Sponsor tracks are optional and require their own qualifications.
- Online entries are **recorded-demo submissions** and require a video link. The form accepts Loom, YouTube, or an X post containing a video. In-person entrants are strongly encouraged to provide a recording too.
- A project link, name, description, presenter name, contact email, and presentation mode are required. Repository and X-post fields are optional at form level; sponsor rules may differ.
- Bankr API integration is optional. A planned project token must use Bankr launch/funding/distribution. This kit plans no token.

The screenshots do not specify video duration, AI-voice policy, prior-work eligibility, testnet acceptance, permission to submit multiple separate projects, or the final review-page confirmations. Do not invent those rules. Screenshots are the evidence basis; a JavaScript-only website fetch does not supersede them.
