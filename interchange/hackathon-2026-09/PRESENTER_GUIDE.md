## Dynamic recording — reserve the real proof segment

Show the user task and budget; run free versus current-source selection; show the malicious-source refusal; then record the separately approved Dynamic wallet action and its checked outcome. The approved recipient, amount, network, Dynamic SDK/pattern and transaction evidence must be legible. Do not expose credentials, MPC shares, raw usable signatures, or private operator files.

Until that action is performed, label footage **prototype preview / wallet action not demonstrated**. Do not animate a fake confirmation or narrate a fixture as a real payment. The existing browser mode is a rehearsal, and the Node local-reference adapter is not production isolation. A merged PR is not a real-provider test.

## Voiceover

The screenshots require a video but do not specify human narration. Prefer the founder's own calm voice for authenticity; a clearly identified generic AI narrator is another production option, subject to organizer policy. No voice cloning or imitation is needed. Do not claim sponsor acceptance of AI narration without confirmation.

Record in short segments while following the caption/script timing. Leave a short pause between segments. Use a quiet room and consistent microphone distance. Listen once for names, numbers and whether words claim more than the visible evidence. Captions remain useful with either voice. A silent captioned draft is not a claim that narration was generated.

## Online submission

Export an ordinary MP4 recording and upload it to a video host accepted by the form: Loom, YouTube, or an X post containing the video. MP4/H.264 is the recommended compatibility format, not a form-stated codec rule. Verify playback, audio and captions while signed out, on a second device, and without permission requests. Paste that public/viewable video URL into the form. An HTML demo is the project link; a GIF or text post is not the sole recording deliverable.

## Rehearsal acceptance

Complete three timed fresh-start walkthroughs on the actual presentation machine. Record revision, date, duration and failures. Test one network failure and use the dated fallback honestly. A challenge/429 means stop, not bypass; a changed freeze means hold, not reconfigure; a wallet approval in the Bankr inspection demo means cancel. No new credentials, payments, signing, deployment or automatic retry is authorized by this guide.
