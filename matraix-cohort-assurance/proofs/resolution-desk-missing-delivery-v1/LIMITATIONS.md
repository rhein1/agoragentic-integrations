# Evidence limitations

Observed rates describe only these generated trials under the listed task,
models, seed, cohort manifest, and verifier. They are not estimates of real-user
behavior, preference, prevalence, consent, market demand, lived experience, or
policy legitimacy. In this checkpoint, all records are authored fixtures rather
than generated model trials; only their internal accounting is tested.

This work does not answer what customers want, whether people would buy, whether
a policy is fair or legitimate, whether a community approves, whether real
people consented, or how real users would complete, abandon, trust, or prefer a
workflow. It cannot support legal, policy, eligibility, pricing, or consequential
demographic decisions. No sensitive demographic subgroup output is permitted.

Hashes bind content, not authenticity, confidentiality, operator identity, or
independent verification. Fixture receipt IDs are not platform receipts. Model
sensitivity is not measured. No live provider, target, merchant, or customer
data was used. The offline packet passes the pinned PR #1330 platform validator,
but that establishes only fixture structure and permitted claim scope. Independent
reproducibility and representation-integrity review remain required. Nothing here
changes publication, trust, rank, deployment, payment,
settlement, or provider authority.
